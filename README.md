# ⚖️ Contract Risk Explainer

> An Explainable AI application that analyzes legal contracts, identifies potentially risky clauses, and explains why they may be problematic in clear, understandable language.

Developed during the **Lighthouse Umeå 2025 Innovation Competition**, the project leverages Large Language Models (LLMs) and Explainable AI (XAI) principles to make contract review more transparent and accessible for non-legal professionals.

Instead of simply flagging risks, the application explains the reasoning behind each assessment, helping users better understand contractual obligations, hidden risks, and ambiguous language.

---

# ✨ Features

- 📄 Upload legal contracts
- 🤖 AI-powered clause analysis
- ⚠️ Detection of potentially risky contract clauses
- 💬 Plain-language explanations for identified risks
- 🔍 Explainable AI approach instead of black-box predictions
- 📑 Clause-by-clause review
- 🎯 Risk prioritization
- ⚖️ Improved transparency for AI-generated assessments
- 💻 Modern responsive web interface

---

# 💡 Motivation

Legal contracts are often lengthy, complex, and difficult to interpret without legal expertise.

Traditional AI systems may identify problematic clauses but frequently fail to explain *why* they are considered risky, reducing user trust and limiting practical adoption.

Contract Risk Explainer addresses this challenge by combining modern Large Language Models with Explainable AI principles to provide understandable, contextual explanations alongside every identified risk.

The goal is not to replace legal professionals, but to assist users in understanding contracts before seeking expert advice.

---

# 🏗️ How It Works

The application follows a simple workflow:

```text
Contract Upload
        │
        ▼
 Text Extraction
        │
        ▼
 Clause Segmentation
        │
        ▼
 LLM Risk Analysis
        │
        ▼
 Explainable AI Layer
        │
        ▼
 User-Friendly Report
```

Each clause is independently evaluated and accompanied by an explanation describing:

- Why the clause may be risky
- What legal implications it may have
- Which wording triggered the assessment
- Suggested points requiring further attention

---

# 🧠 Explainable AI

Unlike traditional AI systems that only provide predictions, this project emphasizes **transparency**.

Each detected issue includes:

- Clear reasoning
- Context-aware explanations
- Human-readable language
- Traceable evidence from the contract itself

This helps users understand *why* the AI reached its conclusions instead of blindly trusting a risk score.
